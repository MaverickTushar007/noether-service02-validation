**What the study tests.** The model trades on the open after a completed SMA signal, uses 5 bp per fill, and earns zero on idle SMA cash. A separate reviewer reconciled the holdings, fees and daily equity. The fixed benchmark targets 66.587695% SPY monthly using development data. The remaining sleeve earns modeled historical Treasury accrual. A shared accrual sensitivity would help isolate cash economics from timing, but requires authorization.

**Why the decision remains open.** Lower holdout volatility did not translate into a shallower maximum drawdown than the fixed mix. The SMA trailed both SPY benchmarks on cumulative return. The paired interval spans zero. One asset and one holdout do not resolve selection bias or establish generalization. Dividend ex-date cash availability and opening execution assumptions remain unverified.

**For Roosevelt.** Present reproducibility, statistical reliability and economic value as separate decisions. Require research provenance, implementable execution evidence and a measurable hurdle in a future engagement. Decide whether to authorize matched cash or settlement sensitivities under a new protocol.
