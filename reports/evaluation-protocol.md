# Phase 5 prespecified benchmark evaluation

Fixed before calculating holdout performance. Earlier development results were known. The evaluation does not revise earlier findings or the trading rule.

## Scope and boundary

Use the frozen SPY CSV and XNYS sessions. Development runs 2011-01-03 through 2018-12-31. Holdout uses the sessions within 2019-01-01 through 2022-12-31. All four portfolios start each window independently with $100,000 USD, zero shares and no prior cash accrual. On the first holdout open, the SMA portfolio may act on the target formed at the last 2018 close, using only the final 50 completed 2018 closes to initialize its windows. Development starts without warmup history and starts cash. Each window marks its final holdings at the last close without forced liquidation. No new SPY download. The holdout execution command claims an exclusive run marker before parsing holdout prices. Test fixtures and the development reconciliation run before this gate. A claimed run cannot be repeated by changing output paths.

## Portfolio rules

- SMA 21/50 uses completed daily provider Close with automatic adjustment disabled. Long if the fast SMA strictly exceeds the slow SMA. Warmup or ties target cash. Exposure changes fill at the next session's provider Open. Buy with all cash inclusive of costs and sell all shares on exit. Fractional shares, zero interest on all SMA idle cash, no leverage, no shorts.
- SPY buy and hold buys at the first open, budgeted inclusive of fees. It retains all shares and reinvests available distribution cash, including accrued interest, at the next session open after the distribution. Same-day distributions are credited after the opening trade and can only be reinvested at a later open. This uses a common modeled opening execution and avoids using a dividend to size its own ex-date purchase.
- Historical cash holds the modeled DTB3 cash proxy. No SPY trades and no trading fee.
- Fixed mix targets the exact verified Phase 4 mean closing invested weight of 0.6658769500542819 in SPY. Rebalance at the first open of each calendar month and at the first open of each window. Size using opening equity inclusive of contemporaneously available cash and costs so that post-fee SPY weight equals target. Between rebalances weights drift. The cash sleeve earns historical proxy interest. This matches one development exposure statistic. Beta, exposure timing, realized weight and risk can differ.

All SPY fills pay 5 bp of absolute traded notional, including first buys, reinvestments and rebalances. Use unrestricted fractional shares, no extra spread, market impact, taxes or settlement delay. All portfolios credit dividends and capital gains on provider event dates to prior-close shares. The SMA and fixed mix may use that model cash on the event open. Buy and hold defers reinvestment one open by convention. No opening buyer receives the event credit. No adjusted appreciation or forced exit. Reject unsupported splits.

## Cash proxy and Sharpe

Use the separate frozen FRED DTB3 daily 3-month Treasury bill secondary-market rate on a bank-discount basis. Convert the percentage d to a 91-day bill price P=1-(d/100)×91/360 and an effective calendar-day factor P^(-1/91). Multiply prior close cash by that factor raised to the number of calendar days to the next session, then subtract prior cash for interest. On each accrual interval use the latest nonmissing rate dated strictly before the preceding SPY session date so that same-date and later releases cannot affect that interval. Carry the available rate over holidays and blank observations. Fail if stale by more than 10 calendar days as measured at the preceding session. This lagged, revised-vintage yield proxy is synthetic accrual, without bill price marks, maturity rolls, actual trading costs or a point-in-time vintage guarantee. Ex-date spendability is a modeling approximation. Cash proxy interval returns are the common risk-free return for all Sharpes. Zero sample variance of excess return means undefined Sharpe. The SMA's idle balance remains at the original zero rate intentionally. Benchmark cash uses rate accrual.

## Measures and judgment

Use each session close over preceding equity (initial cash on day one). Report cumulative return, CAGR using 252/n, annual volatility and excess Sharpe using sample standard deviations and sqrt(252), positive running-peak drawdown and maximum consecutive underwater sessions, including any ongoing drawdown and using initial capital as a peak. Also report closing exposure (average SPY value/equity and fraction of long closes), gross turnover (absolute fill notional/prior equity summed, also annualized at 252), fee totals, trades, distributions and interest. Do not force a year-end liquidation. Report descriptive window cumulative returns using the preceding mark as base. Freeze 2019 calendar year, 2020-02-19 through 2020-03-23 selloff, 2020-03-24 through 2020-12-31 rebound, and calendar 2022. These named historical windows are contextual and excluded from inference.

Primary comparator is fixed mix. Secondary comparators are buy and hold and historical cash. For each H0 is zero mean aligned daily net return difference (SMA minus benchmark), with two-sided alternative. For the primary holdout comparison use a 95% percentile noncircular moving-block interval, 20 trading sessions per block, 20,000 draws, PCG64 seed 20261127 reset for each pair. Resample paired return differences, with replacement over contiguous block starts, truncating to original n. Report daily and 252× arithmetic annualized differences and intervals, and the difference in separately compounded total returns. Report the same intervals descriptively for secondary comparisons and development, with no multiple-testing claims. No bootstrap of raw equity or separate unpaired resampling.

Primary economic criterion PASS requires a strictly positive lower primary mean-difference bound and positive compounded return gap. FAIL requires a strictly negative upper bound and negative compounded return gap. All other outcomes are INCONCLUSIVE. These screens use zero incremental return as the minimum hurdle and do not establish deployability. Timing and accounting PASS requires exact date and signal availability and independent daily reconciliation. Realistic cash implementation remains INCONCLUSIVE where the synthetic proxy cannot establish broker or bill total returns. Benchmark differences cannot identify a causal timing effect. Preserve all results and distinguish exposure, timing and uncertainty.
