# Phase 4 statistical validation protocol

Written before any Phase 4 calculations or tests. The reviewer has seen Phase 3 development results. This protocol fixes Phase 4 choices prospectively within this development exercise and carries no claim of a blind confirmatory study.

## Inputs and timing

Use SPY development sessions from 2011-01-01 through 2018-12-31. Verify all recorded SHA-256 references from the Phase 1, 2 and 3 manifests. Use saved Phase 2 equity and fills for primary summary statistics and only frozen development bars for sensitivity and regime calculations. Reading whole files for hashing is permitted. Never parse out-of-scope market prices for analysis, evaluate holdout strategy results or write to prior artifacts.

Use provider Close with automatic adjustment disabled. At each completed exchange close form the 21/50 target, strictly greater for long. Orders are eligible at the next scheduled session open. Verify every recorded target transition and fill, cross-check reported Open and closing exposure, and probe prefix invariance by changing future prices. Challenge a same-close fill, a prematurely released weekly or monthly label, and an invalid numeric value beyond the development boundary. Exchange session labels and event ordering establish model availability. Vendor publication latency and historical order feasibility remain unverified.

Daily, weekly and monthly labels use 21 and 50 observations in their own frequency. Weeks run Monday–Sunday. Months follow the calendar. Period closes are the last scheduled XNYS session close, with every scheduled session in the period required. Include holidays and shortened weeks as the calendar specifies. A completed label can be consumed at the next scheduled open, with no release recorded beyond development. Count completed observations, 49-period warmup when at least 50 observations exist, ready/long/cash labels, transitions (including the first ready long from cash), and next-open releases. Record the first and last ready dates. No regime-conditioned return analysis, clustering claims, blending or additional orders.

## Primary uncertainty calculation

Let r[t] = closing equity[t] / preceding equity - 1 with $100,000 preceding the first session. Include flat days and warmup. Estimate the unconditional population mean of the specified net daily portfolio return within a development-like stationary process. Null H0 is mean daily return = 0 versus two-sided H1 different from zero. For the primary approximate 5% test, reject H0 if zero is outside the 95% percentile interval. Report sign and all intervals. A positive reliability finding for this mean screen requires all three lower bounds strictly above zero. If the primary upper bound is below zero, classify the positive-mean screen FAIL. Otherwise classify it INCONCLUSIVE. Intervals including zero indicate insufficient evidence to distinguish the mean from zero under the model.

Resample daily returns using fixed-length, overlapping non-circular moving blocks. Draw uniform start offsets with replacement, concatenate ceil(n/L) blocks, truncate to n, then take the arithmetic mean. Use 20,000 replicates per length, NumPy PCG64 initialized to seed 20261127 separately for each length, and linear 2.5th and 97.5th percentiles. Primary L=20 approximates one trading month. Sensitivity L=10 and L=40 halve and double the primary length. Calculate arithmetic annualization by multiplying daily mean and interval endpoints by 252. Report geometric CAGR separately as (ending / initial)^(252/n) - 1. No bootstrap of equity levels. Idle cash interest is zero. Within-block dependence is retained, but stationarity, adequate sample size and limited dependence are assumptions, and blocks break dependence across joins. The intervals are conditional on the fixed rule, chosen sample and bootstrap design. No multiple testing or selection inference is claimed. See [Künsch (1989)](https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176347265).

Report fraction of closes holding shares, mean close position value divided by equity, and gross daily turnover as sum of absolute fill notionals divided by prior close equity ($100,000 on day one). Report sum over all days and annualized daily mean × 252. Gross counts both buys and sells in full.

## Exploratory diagnostics

Parameter cases in declared order at 5 bp per fill are 18/45, 21/50, 24/55. Cost cases with 21/50 are 5, 10 and 20 bp per fill. Preserve the reference, next-open scheduling, full fractional fills, cost-inclusive sizing, no idle interest, and ex-date cash credits. Use the existing client calculations plus the Phase 3 independent Decimal calculations for each unique case. Mark parameters exploratory and costs illustrative. Report fills, annualized gross turnover, closing exposure, costs, cumulative return, arithmetic annualized mean, CAGR and drawdown. Show full path accounting checks. No ranking, recommendation, model selection or joint grid.

## Findings and limitations

Integrity PASS means all recorded hashes match. Timing PASS means recorded dates and open prices reconcile, prefix checks and controlled counterexamples pass, and scope is bounded. Bootstrap implementation PASS means the finite return series and reference computation check out and reproducible intervals are generated. Diagnostic and regime implementation PASS requires predefined cases, independent agreement or exact release chronology, and reconciliation. Any observed violation of these checkable criteria is FAIL. A successful implementation is separate from evidence that mean return is positive. Predictive reliability and incremental value versus passive exposure are INCONCLUSIVE without untouched evaluation, external selection provenance, and a prespecified risk or benchmark comparison. No historical research log establishes how SPY, the dates or SMA parameters were picked. Single-asset selection, development reuse, provider revisions, survivorship/generalization limits, settlement timing and assumed fills all constrain interpretation.
